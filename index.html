What Is IBS?

Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) is a common digestive disorder that affects the large intestine. Symptoms often include bloating, abdominal pain, gas, diarrhea, constipation, or a combination of these symptoms.

While IBS can significantly affect quality of life, many people successfully manage their symptoms through dietary changes, stress management, and regular exercise.

Understanding the Low FODMAP Diet

The Low FODMAP diet helps identify foods that may trigger IBS symptoms. FODMAPs are certain carbohydrates that can be difficult to digest for some individuals.

Many people find symptom relief by reducing high-FODMAP foods and gradually reintroducing them under guidance.

  • ✅ Chicken
  • ✅ Eggs
  • ✅ Rice
  • ✅ Potatoes
  • ✅ Carrots
  • ✅ Spinach
  • ✅ Strawberries
  • ✅ Lactose-Free Dairy
